# GrowLocal OS — Sales Playbook

## 30-second pitch
GrowLocal helps local businesses turn marketing into a simple growth system. We identify the biggest revenue opportunities, build a 30-day action plan, create campaign ideas and content, track progress, and package the results into clear monthly reporting — all from one workspace.

## Ideal first customers
Start with owner-operated businesses where the owner can make a buying decision quickly: cafés, restaurants, salons, barbers, gyms, clinics, trades, independent retail, and local service businesses.

## Offer
- Setup: £199
- Growth Partner: £149/month
- Premium managed service: £349/month

The initial service can be delivered manually through GrowLocal OS while the production integrations are being built.

## Discovery questions
1. What is the business trying to grow over the next 90 days?
2. Which days, services, products, or customer segments are underperforming?
3. How are leads or repeat customers currently followed up?
4. Which marketing channels are currently used consistently?
5. What would make this service obviously worth paying for each month?

## Demo flow
1. Open the prospect's client workspace.
2. Show the growth score and opportunity estimate.
3. Open the 30-day action plan.
4. Generate example content/campaign ideas.
5. Show the monthly report.
6. Finish with the proposal and next action.

## Close
“We can set the system up around your business, build the first 30-day plan, and manage the growth workflow with you. The setup is £199 and ongoing support starts at £149 per month.”

## Rule
Do not promise guaranteed revenue. Sell clarity, execution, consistency, reporting, and measurable experiments.

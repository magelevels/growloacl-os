## Product

GrowLocal is a growth operating system and productized growth service for independent local businesses. It turns scattered marketing activity into a practical operating rhythm across local discovery, conversion, repeat visits and reputation.

The product combines a free growth-audit funnel with a client workspace and a protected admin sales workspace. Its working promise is a clear next move: one commercial goal, one owner, one deadline and one proof point.

## Audience

- Owner-operators and small teams at cafés, salons, gyms, trades, studios and restaurants.
- Businesses with existing demand but inconsistent visibility, enquiries, repeat visits or review momentum.
- Buyers who want plain-English priorities and practical support instead of another dashboard full of vanity metrics.

## Problem and desired outcome

Local businesses often have activity across search, social, offers and reviews without a shared commercial priority. They need to know which gap matters most, what to do next and how to tell whether it worked.

GrowLocal helps them move from scattered activity to a measurable 30-day execution rhythm and a clearer path to the next customer.

## Positioning

For independent local businesses that need practical growth support, GrowLocal is a growth operating system that connects discovery, conversion, retention and reputation into one next-move workflow. Unlike channel-only tools or generic marketing reports, GrowLocal scores the whole customer path and turns the biggest gap into an owned, time-bound action.

## Differentiation

- **Whole-path view:** visibility, conversion, repeat visits and reputation are reviewed together.
- **Next-move discipline:** recommendations are written as an owner, deadline and proof point.
- **Plain-English commercial focus:** the service connects activity to customers, repeat visits, spend and trust.
- **Practical entry point:** the free audit identifies the next best move before a paid commitment.

These are product principles, not verified customer-result claims. There are no approved testimonials, logos or benchmark claims in the current repository.

## Offer and pricing

- **Free growth audit:** the primary conversion action; no obligation and no mailing-list signup.
- **Growth Setup — £199 one-off:** a 90-day commercial plan and first action plan.
- **Growth Partner — £149/month:** monthly scorecard, planning and optimisation rhythm.
- **Done-With-You — £349/month:** hands-on execution support for campaigns and follow-up.

The audit should help a buyer choose the level of support that fits the next move. Pricing and packaging remain subject to customer validation.

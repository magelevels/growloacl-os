Privacy

This notice explains how GrowLocal handles information submitted through the free growth audit form and the live product demo.

Growth audit requests

When you request an audit, we collect your name, work email, business name, business type, location, growth challenge, and any optional website and customer-range information you provide. We use this information to review the business, respond to the request and discuss relevant GrowLocal services. We do not use the form to add you to an unrelated mailing list.

Storage and sharing

Audit requests are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database used by GrowLocal. Cloudflare processes hosting, security and database data on our behalf. We do not sell submitted information. Access to leads is not exposed through a public website endpoint.

Retention and your choices

Audit-request information should be retained only while it is needed to respond, manage a prospective client relationship and meet applicable business or legal requirements. You can ask us to correct or delete your submitted information. Before commercial promotion begins, GrowLocal must add a monitored contact email and full business-controller details here.

Demo workspace data

Information entered into the live demo is stored locally in your browser. It is not designed for confidential, sensitive or production customer information. Clearing site data can remove it.

Analytics and security logs

No third-party marketing analytics are intentionally configured in this release. Standard network, application and security logs may be processed by Cloudflare while delivering and protecting the service.

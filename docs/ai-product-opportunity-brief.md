# GrowLocal AI product opportunity brief

**Date:** 2026-10-24  
**Purpose:** Identify AI products GrowLocal can build and sell to independent local businesses, then choose a defensible first wedge.

## Executive recommendation

Build **GrowLocal Next-Move Campaigns** first.

> **Proposed USP:** GrowLocal turns one local-business insight into one approved promotion, one owned next action, and one proof of what changed — from first search to repeat visit.

This extends the current GrowLocal product instead of replacing it. The audit identifies the largest gap; the campaign engine turns that gap into a practical promotion or follow-up; the proof ledger records whether enquiries, bookings, repeat visits or reviews changed.

The important distinction is that AI recommends and drafts, while deterministic workflows, approvals and measurement control what gets sent. This is more credible for local owners than an autonomous “AI employee” making unreviewed promises.

## Why the first wedge should be Next-Move Campaigns

1. It fits the existing GrowLocal audit, pipeline, proposal and deadline workflow.
2. It creates an obvious before/after story: “we found this gap, ran this action, and observed this change.”
3. It works with low-risk drafts and approval before any external message is sent.
4. It can begin without deep phone or POS integrations: generate assets, tracked links and tasks first; connect Google, WhatsApp, calendars and POS systems in stages.
5. It gives every later product a home. Lead Rescue, Repeat Visit and Reviews become campaign triggers and evidence sources rather than disconnected add-ons.

## Product shape

### The weekly loop

1. **Detect:** combine audit fields, lead stage, next-action date, review themes, recent campaign activity and customer recency.
2. **Choose:** select the highest-value gap using transparent rules, then ask AI to draft the recommendation.
3. **Approve:** owner sees the audience, message, offer, channel, expiry, expected outcome and risk flags.
4. **Launch:** publish or export to the selected channel with consent and frequency controls.
5. **Measure:** record enquiries, bookings, redemptions, repeat visits, review changes and owner notes.
6. **Learn:** use the result to improve the next recommendation without making unsupported performance claims.

### The guardrails that sell trust

- AI suggestions are drafts until an authorised user approves them.
- No invented prices, availability, discounts, testimonials or performance results.
- Consent, opt-out and channel frequency are first-class fields.
- Every external action has an audit log with the person or workflow that approved it.
- Spend, refunds, regulated advice and sensitive complaints always escalate to a human.
- Tenant data is isolated; credentials are scoped per integration and revocable.

## Recommended packaging

- **Free audit:** identify the largest growth gap and show one example next move.
- **Next Move — £79/month:** one active campaign, weekly brief, tracked link, approval workflow and proof ledger.
- **Growth Operator — £149/month:** three active campaigns, lead rescue, review workflow and repeat-visit segments.
- **Done-With-You — £349/month:** setup, monthly review, campaign configuration and human optimisation.
- Charge message, voice and third-party platform costs separately and visibly.

These prices are test points, not final claims. Validate them with five to ten owner interviews and paid pilots.

## 30/60/90-day build plan

### Days 0–30: prove the loop

- Add campaign, approval, campaign-run, conversion-event and consent records to D1.
- Add an admin “Generate next move” action using deterministic gap rules plus AI copy drafting.
- Output a campaign brief, email/social/Google copy, tracked link and follow-up task.
- Keep launch manual or export-only until the approval and evidence model is tested.
- Measure time-to-approve, campaigns launched and recorded outcomes.

### Days 31–60: connect distribution

- Add Google Business Profile OAuth and posts/offers where eligible.
- Add WhatsApp or email delivery with opt-in, templates, opt-out and rate limits.
- Add campaign status, expiry and basic redemption/booking capture.
- Add owner-ready weekly brief and proof ledger views.

### Days 61–90: add retention

- Add repeat-visit segments and review-triggered follow-up.
- Connect one booking or POS provider first, based on the strongest customer segment.
- Add vertical playbooks for one high-frequency niche, such as salons, cafés or home services.
- Run a paid pilot and publish only verified results.

## What not to build first

- A generic AI chatbot with no business context or action path.
- A full voice receptionist before pricing, escalation and trust rules are proven.
- A broad social scheduler that competes feature-for-feature with established suites.
- A dashboard that adds more metrics without a decision, owner and deadline.
- Autonomous outbound marketing without consent, frequency limits and approval.
